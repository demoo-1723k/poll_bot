7.1.1 Robotic Sensing
Robotic sensing means the ability of a robot to
collect information from itself and from its
surrounding environment.
What does sensing do?
Measures internal state: speed, joint angle,
battery and motor current.
Measures external environment: objects,
distance, light, sound and location.
Converts physical signals into digital data for
algorithms.
Common sensors
Camera, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensor, IMU, GPS,
encoder, force/torque sensor, tactile sensor and
microphone.

7.1.2 Manipulation
Manipulation is the ability of a robot to
physically interact with objects.
Reach an object using joints and links.
Grasp the object using a gripper or hand.
Move, place, assemble, cut, weld or sort it.
Control position, speed and force.

7.2 Navigation and Path Planning
Navigation means the ability of a robot to move from one place to another safely.
Path planning means choosing the best or safest route from the starting point to the goal.
Navigation problem
A robot must answer three questions:
1 Where am I?
2 Where is the goal?
3 How can I move there safely?
Localization estimating the robot’s
current position..
Mapping builds a model of the
environment.
Path planning chooses a route.
Obstacle avoidance reacts to changes.

7.2.1 Autonomous Robotic Systems
An autonomous robot can perform tasks with
limited or no direct human control.
Autonomy levels
Manual: human controls every action.
Assisted: The robot supports the human, but
the human is still the main decision-maker.
Supervised autonomy:The human gives the
goal, and the robot decides how to complete it.
Full autonomy: robot senses, plans and acts
independently.

Summary
Robotics combines sensing, decision-making and physical action.
Sensors allow robots to perceive internal and external conditions.
Manipulation allows robots to grasp, move and interact with objects.
HRI focuses on safe and useful communication between humans and robots.
Navigation and path planning allow robots to move toward goals while avoiding obstacles.
AI gives robots perception, learning, reasoning and adaptive behavior.
